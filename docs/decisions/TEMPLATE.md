# NNN — Short decision title

Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNN

## Problem

What was actually broken or missing. Cite the concrete symptom (a bug
report, a failure mode, a gap), not a vague "code quality" complaint.

## Investigation

What you found tracing it back — logs, spec/docs read, code paths ruled
out. This is the part that proves it's a real root cause, not a guess.
Link to the source of truth if one exists (an OpenAPI spec, a platform doc,
a prior incident).

## Options considered

1. **Option A** — what it would involve, why it was or wasn't enough.
2. **Option B** — same.

## Decision

What was actually implemented, and why this option over the others.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it doesn't solve, any follow-up work it
implies. It's fine to list a real trade-off here — a decision with no
downsides usually means the trade-offs weren't examined.

## Verification

How this was confirmed to work — ideally a regression test (link it), or
the manual repro steps if a test wasn't practical.
